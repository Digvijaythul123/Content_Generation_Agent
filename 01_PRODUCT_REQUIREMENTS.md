# Product Requirements Document

## Goal
Automate Instagram content operations while keeping human approval mandatory.

## Users
Primary user: Page owner.

## Functional Requirements
1. Generate topics
2. Generate scripts
3. Generate captions
4. Generate hashtags
5. Store content
6. Approve/reject content
7. Schedule posts
8. Analyze performance

## Success Metrics
- 30+ posts/month
- <30 minutes review per week
- Consistent posting cadence
