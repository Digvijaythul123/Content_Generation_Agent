TITLE:
The Education Debt Fallacy

HOOK:
Stop treating your degree as an asset. It’s actually a depreciating liability.

SCRIPT:
(Visual: Fast cuts of a graduation cap, a corporate cubicle, and a ticking clock)
Most Indian professionals view their degree as a lifelong shield. 
But in a first-principles world, a degree is just a receipt for a transaction that happened years ago.
The market doesn't pay you for what you learned in 2015; it pays you for the problems you can solve today.
When you rely on your credentials to get you promoted, you are betting on a dead asset.
The most dangerous place to be is "over-qualified" but "under-skilled."
Your degree got you the interview. Your ability to unlearn gets you the wealth.

CAPTION:
Your degree has an expiration date. Are you still relying on a receipt from five years ago to negotiate your value today? Stop collecting credentials and start solving problems. #RationalThoughts #CareerStrategy #FirstPrinciples #CorporateIndia
